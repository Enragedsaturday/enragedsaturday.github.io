# Fixture Related Home

*What is the related fixture rule?*

> [!rule] Black-letter rule
> **Related fixture rule.** The related fixture rule governs the neighboring scenario. *[[Zoning Board v. Doe]]*, 500 U.S. 1 (1991).

## The Brief

The related fixture rule is the black-letter statement this page owns.
