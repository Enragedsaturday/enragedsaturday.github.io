# LINT-15 required-section fixture (fail)

*Is this enough to search?*

> [!rule] Black-letter rule
> **Probable cause** is a fair probability that evidence will be found.
> *[[Illinois v. Gates|Gates]]*, 462 U.S. 213, 238 (1983). ^rule-probable-cause

## The Brief

Probable cause is the practical, non-technical quantum required for a full search.
