## Body

The reasoning traces back to Terry v. Ohio, a stop-and-frisk landmark left bare here.

Compare the harmless-error line in Chapman v. California, which has no page and must stay plain.

A linked mention such as [[Terry v. Ohio]] is fine, and a markdown link
[Terry v. Ohio](https://www.courtlistener.com/opinion/1/terry-v-ohio/) is not a bare caption.

Quoted opinion text like "the officer relied on Terry v. Ohio" is exempt.
