# LINT-3 A9 pass fixture

## The Brief

A compliant Explanation-layer paragraph makes one doctrinal move and invokes at
most three cases: the rule traces from *[[Katz v. United States|Katz]]* through
*[[Terry v. Ohio|Terry]]* to *[[United States v. Jones|Jones]]*, and stops there.

A case wall correctly rendered as labeled bullets does NOT fire A9 (bullets are
the sanctioned form even with many cases):

- **[[Katz v. United States|Katz]]** — the reasonable-expectation test.
- **[[Terry v. Ohio|Terry]]** — the stop-and-frisk standard.
- **[[United States v. Jones|Jones]]** — trespass revival.
- **[[Carpenter v. United States|Carpenter]]** — the digital exception.
