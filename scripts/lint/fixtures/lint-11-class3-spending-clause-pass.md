## Brief

RLUIPA rests on Congress's Spending Clause power. The Spending Clause lets
Congress attach conditions to federal funds, and a funding recipient is bound
only by conditions to which it knowingly agreed. A Spending Clause statute
therefore reaches only consenting recipients. This regression fixture guards
the P4-17(d) word-boundary fix: the term of art above must not fire the status
pattern, because its leading letter blocks the marker's left boundary.
