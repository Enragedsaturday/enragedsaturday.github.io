# LINT-16 Historical role-prefix fixture (pass)

RULING P4-18(iii): a **Historical foil.** Key-cases role prefix (adjective + a
lowercase continuation word) is descriptive prose, not a leaked S1 A8 tier-6
weight label. The weight-label detector must NOT fire on it, while it keeps
firing on a standalone/annotated `Historical` leak (see lint-16-historical-fail).
