# Doctrine Fixture Page

**Rule 1 — name-half already pin-deep.** The license is defined by custom: "the quoted holding of the case appears here in full." *[[Fixture v. Sample#^pin-6|Sample]]*, 569 U.S. at 6 (2013).

**Rule 2 — page-level name-half plus a quotation to upgrade.** As the Court put it, "a second quoted passage sits on the very next page." *[[Fixture v. Sample|Sample]]*, 569 U.S. at 9.

**Rule 3 — paraphrase, no quotation.** The Court read the license narrowly and confined it to ordinary visitors. *[[Fixture v. Sample|Sample]]*, 569 U.S. at 12.

**Zone guard.** Inside a direct quotation a pincite is never rewritten: "see 569 U.S. at 6 for the point." And in a heading the same is true.
