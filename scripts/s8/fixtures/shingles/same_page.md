# Same Page Restatement

> [!rule] Black-letter rule
> A frisk is a limited protective search of the outer clothing for weapons, justified only when an officer can point to specific and articulable facts warranting a reasonable belief that the suspect is armed and presently dangerous.
> ^rule-self
