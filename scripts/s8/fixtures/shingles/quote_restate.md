# Quote Restatement

The controlling passage from that case is worth quoting in full:

> As the Supreme Court explained, "the warrantless entry into a private dwelling to conduct a routine felony arrest crosses the firm line drawn at the entrance to the house and cannot be justified absent exigent circumstances or valid consent from an occupant."

The block quote above re-types a passage the case page already pins, which is exactly the pinned-quote flavor the detector must flag even though the text sits inside a direct quotation.
