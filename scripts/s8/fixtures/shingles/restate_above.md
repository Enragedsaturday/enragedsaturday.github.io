# Restatement Above Threshold

On this page we teach the same doctrine as the home page. The government conducts a search when it physically intrudes upon a constitutionally protected area for the purpose of gathering information, and such an intrusion is presumptively unreasonable unless the officers first obtain a warrant supported by probable cause and particularity. That restatement is what the detector should catch.
