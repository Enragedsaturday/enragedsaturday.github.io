## Per-item reasoning (disagreements, flags, overrulings only)

### Items 5 & 6 — Reed v. Goertz, Williams v. Reed (lanes unanimous EXCLUDE vs. orchestrator INGEST-bullet)

Both blind lanes said EXCLUDE; the original packet recommended INGEST as in-scope-non-page bullets.
The rule text sides with the lanes. An "in-scope-non-page bullet" is still an **INGEST**
disposition, and R2 makes INGEST conditional on a prong: (a) field conduct — none (postconviction
DNA-process timing; benefits-administration exhaustion); (b) admissibility — none; (c) **officer
civil-liability exposure** — the prong is exposure doctrine, not §1983-doctrine-at-large. Reed's
accrual rule and Williams' exhaustion-preemption rule govern when and where *plaintiffs* may sue
benefit administrators and DAs; neither moves what an officer must know about his own exposure.
The D1 flip cannot rescue either: it requires a gate-passing named-in-prose case, and neither is
named anywhere in `content/` (verified). The corpus's accrual thread is the Heck
favorable-termination line; its no-exhaustion thread is Monroe, stated twice on the §1983 page
(:24 body, :66 table — verified). EXCLUDE, remits named: **§1983/postconviction civil-litigation
timing craft** (Reed) and **§1983 forum/exhaustion litigation mechanics** (Williams). The
orchestrator's bullet recommendation is recorded and declined — no rule supplies an INGEST-without-
a-prong disposition.

### Item 7 — Price v. Montgomery County (fable INGEST-mention vs codex EXCLUDE)

Resolved **against codex**. Codex's two grounds both fail:

1. *"Prosecutor immunity is not the officer civil-liability exposure covered by R2(c)."* The
   corpus's §1983 immunity architecture already carries the absolute-immunity tier as an
   adjudicated in-remit hole-fill: `imbler-v-pachtman--109387`, `buckley-v-fitzsimmons--112894`,

   absolute immunity ends and the **officer standard (QI)** begins — investigative-function

   exact boundary. That is prong-(c) structure, not trial advocacy.
2. *"No content hit."* Expected — Price is the candidate; the test is whether the corpus's
   carried architecture uses it, and it does (Imbler/Buckley tier + Brady/Connick
   evidence-destruction line).

page** — which is all fable claimed and all this adjudication grants. Web-verified: cert denied
**July 2, 2024** (No. 23-649), Sotomayor statement re absolute immunity's "quite sparing" bounds
(supremecourt.gov 23-649_5i26.pdf; LII; Volokh). ITEMS' "2025" date is wrong.
