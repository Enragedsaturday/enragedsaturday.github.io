## Frontier-split framing (2 rows) — honest split posture named in Treatment (LINT-21 binding-language)
- **McDonough v. Smith** (role: *Illustrates a circuit split*). Taught precisely: **binding** SCOTUS on **accrual** (a § 1983 fabricated-evidence claim accrues on favorable termination), but the Court **expressly reserved** the claim's constitutional source (Fourth Amendment vs. Due Process) and its elements — an issue on which the circuits remain **divided** (the Thomas/Kagan/Gorsuch dissent objected the Court fixed accrual without defining the claim). Treatment names the live split without overclaiming settledness.
- **Northrup v. City of Toledo Police Dept** (role: *Illustrates a circuit split*, 6th Cir.). Rendered as an **in-circuit** rule (binding only in the 6th Cir., persuasive elsewhere): lawful open carry, standing alone, does not supply reasonable suspicion for a *Terry* stop — with the 4th Cir.'s *United States v. Black* on the same side, and other courts finding reasonable suspicion where firearm possession combines with additional factors. Treatment states the in-circuit/persuasive posture explicitly.
