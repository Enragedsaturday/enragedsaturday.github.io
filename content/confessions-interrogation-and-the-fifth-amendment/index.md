# Confessions, Interrogation & the Fifth Amendment

When a suspect's own statements can be used against him: the due-process limit on coerced confessions and the Fifth Amendment rules that govern questioning in custody.

- [[Due-Process Voluntariness of Confessions]] — the pre-Miranda line that still governs: testing whether coercion overbore the suspect's will.
- [[Miranda and Custodial Interrogation]] — when warnings are required, and what counts as custody and as interrogation.
- [[Miranda Waiver and Invocation]] — what happens after the warnings: waiving the rights, or invoking the right to silence or to counsel.
- [[Public-Employee Compelled Statements (Garrity)]] — the limits on using statements a public employee was compelled to give under threat of losing the job.
