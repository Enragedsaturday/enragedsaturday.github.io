# Foundations & the Fourth Amendment

The constitutional and historical bedrock of everything that follows: where the Fourth Amendment came from, what it protects, the order in which to work a search-or-seizure problem, and how the doctrine keeps being re-fitted to new technology.

- [[Common Law Origins]] — the English and colonial roots of the Amendment: general warrants, the writs of assistance, and the framing-era grievances behind the text.
- [[Fourth Amendment Framework]] — the spine of the analysis: what the Amendment protects, the two definitions of a search, the seizure of persons and property, and the state-action requirement.
- [[Fourth Amendment Analysis Checklist]] — the operational sequence to walk a class through: government action, protected interest, search or seizure, standing, and reasonableness.
- [[Fourth Amendment Recalibration]] — how the same text is repeatedly re-fitted as society and technology change, from the automobile and the wiretap to the digital cases.
