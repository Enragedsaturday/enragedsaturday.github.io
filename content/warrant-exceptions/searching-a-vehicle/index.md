# Searching a Vehicle

The warrantless searches the automobile's mobility and reduced privacy make possible, and the separate administrative and checkpoint searches that reach a vehicle for non-investigative reasons.

- [[Automobile Exception|The Automobile Exception]] — searching a readily mobile vehicle on probable cause, containers included.
- [[SIA Vehicles|Search incident to a recent occupant's arrest]] — when an arrest of a driver or passenger justifies searching the passenger compartment.
- [[Inventory Searches]] — the routine, non-investigative catalog of an impounded vehicle's contents.
- [[Checkpoints and Roadblocks|Checkpoints & Roadblocks]] — the suspicionless stops allowed for limited, non-general-crime purposes.
