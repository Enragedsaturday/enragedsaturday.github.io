# Warrant Exceptions

The recognized justifications for searching without a warrant, organized by the object of the search: the person, the vehicle, the home, and effects, plus consent and the programmatic group.

- **Searching a Person** — [[Search Incident to Arrest|search incident to arrest]] as applied to the arrestee's body, cell phones, and chemical testing.
- **Searching a Vehicle** — warrantless vehicle searches: the automobile exception, the search of a recent occupant, inventory, and checkpoints.
- **Home Entry & Search** — the premises bucket: entering to arrest, the emergency exceptions, protective sweeps, and [[Community Caretaking|community caretaking]].
- [[Searching Effects and Containers|Searching Effects & Containers]] — searching luggage, packages, and other closed containers.
- [[Consent Searches|Consent]] — searches justified by voluntary consent, and the questions of who may give it and how far it reaches.
- **Programmatic & Special-Needs Searches** — reasonableness-balancing searches beyond ordinary law enforcement, and the sovereign's border authority.
- [[Knock-and-Talk]] — the consent-based approach to a home's front door, and where a lawful knock becomes a search.
