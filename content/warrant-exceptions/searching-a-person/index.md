# Searching a Person

Search incident to arrest as applied to the arrestee's own body: the automatic search a lawful custodial arrest allows, and the two areas where the Supreme Court has drawn special lines.

- [[SIA Persons|On the arrestee's person]] — the full search of the body and clothing that a lawful custodial arrest permits.
- [[SIA Cell Phones|Cell phones]] — why a phone found on an arrestee is treated differently from everything else in his pockets.
- [[SIA Alcohol Tests|Alcohol & chemical tests]] — the rules for breath and blood testing incident to a drunk-driving arrest.
