# Home Entry & Search

The premises bucket, where the home's special protection meets the situations that let officers enter without a warrant: making an arrest inside, responding to an emergency, and securing the scene.

- [[Entry to Arrest]] — the warrant rules for crossing a threshold to take someone into custody.
- [[Emergency Aid]] — entry to help an occupant who reasonably appears to need immediate assistance.
- [[Exigent Circumstances and Hot Pursuit|Hot Pursuit]] — chasing a fleeing suspect from a public place into a private one.
- [[Destruction of Evidence]] — entry to prevent the imminent loss of evidence.
- [[Securing the Scene|Protective Sweeps & Securing the Scene]] — the limited sweep for hidden persons and the freeze of a home pending a warrant.
- [[Community Caretaking]] — non-investigative help outside the home, and why it does not license a warrantless home entry.
- [[Fire-Scene Entries]] — the authority of firefighters and investigators to remain at and examine a fire scene.
