# Programmatic & Special-Needs Searches

Searches justified by a special governmental need beyond ordinary criminal investigation, judged by balancing rather than individualized suspicion, together with the sovereign's heightened authority at the border.

- [[Special Needs and Administrative Searches|Special Needs & Administrative]] — checkpoints, schools, probation and parole, drug testing, and administrative inspections.
- [[Border Searches]] — the authority to search people and effects entering the country, and the line between routine and non-routine searches.
