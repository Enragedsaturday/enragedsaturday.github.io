# CSSI — Certified Search & Seizure Instructor

**Federal standard · criminal-suppression focus.** Every doctrine page states the *federal* rule under the wiki's six-tier authority lexicon: **U.S. Supreme Court binding nationwide; a circuit holding binding in-circuit and Persuasive elsewhere (splits flagged); state cases appear only as clearly-labeled illustrations paired with the controlling federal authority.** Every case is verified against CourtListener and checked for good law. The wiki is organized as a **thirteen-category tree** — Foundations & the Fourth Amendment → Standards of Proof → the two threshold pillars (*Searches* and *Seizures*) → the Warrant and its Exceptions → the Exclusionary Rule, Remedies & Standing → Confessions & the Fifth Amendment → the Right to Counsel → Fair-Trial & Reliability Doctrines → Use of Force & Liability → the Legal System & Reference → Instructor Craft — plus a generated [[Case Index]].

## Foundations & the Fourth Amendment

- [[Common Law Origins]] — the English & colonial roots of the Amendment: the general warrant and the writ of assistance; Paxton's Case, *Wilkes v. Wood*, *Entick v. Carrington*, the state declarations of rights, and Madison — grounded in *Boyd* (with *Riley* recounting that founding history).

- [[Fourth Amendment Framework]] — the spine: what the Amendment protects, the **two definitions of "search,"** seizure of property and of persons, the **state-action / private-search** doctrine, and the four-step analysis sequence.

- [[Fourth Amendment Analysis Checklist]] — the operational **checklist**: government action? → protected interest? → search or seizure? → standing? → reasonable (C.R.E.W.)?

- [[Fourth Amendment Recalibration]] — the instructor's frame for how the Amendment is repeatedly re-fitted as technology and society change: the automobile (*Carroll*), the wiretap (*Olmstead* → *Katz*), **incorporation** against the states (*Wolf* 1949 → *Mapp* 1961), and the digital recalibrations (*Kyllo*, *Riley*, *Carpenter*).

## The Exclusionary Rule, Remedies & Standing

- [[The Exclusionary Rule]] — the **suppression remedy**: a judicially-created **deterrent** (not a personal right) barring unlawfully-obtained evidence and its **fruits** (*Wong Sun*; *Weeks*/*Mapp*) — and the exceptions that still let evidence in: **[[Inevitable Discovery and Independent Source|inevitable discovery]]** (*Nix*), **[[Inevitable Discovery and Independent Source|independent source]]** (*Murray*), **[[Fruits and Attenuation|attenuation]]** (*Strieff*), and **good faith** (*Leon*).  ·  *sub-umbrella*
  - [[Fruits and Attenuation|Fruits & Attenuation]] — *placed by S3 — S6 verifies cases, S7 authors prose.*
  - [[The Good-Faith Exception]] — *placed by S3 — S6 verifies cases, S7 authors prose.*
  - [[Inevitable Discovery and Independent Source|Inevitable Discovery & Independent Source]] — *placed by S3 — S6 verifies cases, S7 authors prose.*

- [[Standing to Challenge a Search]] — 4A rights are **personal**: only a defendant whose **own** [[Reasonable Expectation of Privacy|reasonable expectation of privacy]] was invaded may suppress (*Rakas*); possession is not automatic standing (*Rawlings*, *Salvucci*); the overnight guest (*Olson*) vs. the transient visitor (*Carter*); the unauthorized rental driver (*Byrd*).


## Confessions, Interrogation & the Fifth Amendment

- [[Due-Process Voluntariness of Confessions]] — the pre-Miranda line that still governs: a confession produced by coercion that **overbears the will** is inadmissible, judged by the **totality** (*Brown*, *Ashcraft*, *Spano*); coercion must be **police** conduct (*Connelly*).

- [[Miranda and Custodial Interrogation]] — warnings are required before **custodial interrogation**: **custody** (objective — *Berkemer*, *Howes*, *J.D.B.*) and **interrogation** (express questioning or its functional equivalent — *Innis*); the public-safety exception (*Quarles*); *Miranda* is constitutional (*Dickerson*).

- [[Miranda Waiver and Invocation]] — after warnings: **waiver** (express or implied — *Butler*, *Thompkins*) vs. **invocation** — counsel must be unambiguous (*Davis*) and triggers the *Edwards* bar; silence must be scrupulously honored (*Mosley*); the break-in-custody rule (*Shatzer*) and statement-fruits (*Elstad*, *Patane*, *Seibert*).

## The Right to Counsel

- [[Sixth Amendment Right to Counsel]] — attaches at the **initiation of adversary judicial proceedings** (*Kirby*) and is **offense-specific** (*Cobb*); after attachment, **deliberate elicitation** without counsel is barred (*Massiah*, *Brewer v. Williams*, *Henry*); distinct from the 5A *Miranda* right.

## Fair-Trial & Reliability Doctrines

- [[Eyewitness Identification]] — the 6A right to counsel at **post-charge corporeal lineups** (*Wade*/*Gilbert*; not pre-charge *Kirby*, not photos *Ash*) and the due-process bar on **unnecessarily suggestive** identifications judged by reliability (*Biggers*, *Manson*; *Perry*).

- [[Brady and Giglio]] — the prosecution's due-process duty to disclose **[[Brady and Giglio|exculpatory]]** (*Brady*) and **impeachment** (*Giglio*) evidence — including material known only to the **police** (*Kyles*); the "Giglio-impaired" officer.

- [[Entrapment]] — a **substantive defense** (not a suppression doctrine): the federal **subjective** test focuses on the defendant's **predisposition** (*Sorrells*, *Sherman*, *Jacobson*, *Russell*); the government may furnish opportunity but not implant criminal design in an unwilling person.
