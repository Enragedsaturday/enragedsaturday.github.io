# The Warrant

The default rule and its machinery: the Fourth Amendment prefers a warrant, and these pages cover what a valid one requires, how it is obtained, and how it must be carried out.

- [[The Warrant Requirement]] — what a valid warrant demands and the ways one can fail, from the showing of probable cause to particularity.
- **Getting a Warrant** — building an application that survives review: the affidavit, the magistrate, particularity, and the challenge to a false affidavit.
- **Executing a Warrant** — carrying the warrant out lawfully: announcement at the door, control of the people present, and the limits on scope and manner.
