# Getting a Warrant

Building a warrant application that survives later review: the things a magistrate must find before signing, and the challenge a defendant can bring when the affidavit was false.

- [[Probable Cause in the Affidavit]] — what the affidavit must show, including how an informant's tip is weighed.
- [[The Neutral and Detached Magistrate|The Neutral & Detached Magistrate]] — why the issuing magistrate must be neutral, and what disqualifies one.
- [[Particularity]] — the requirement that the warrant describe with precision the place to be searched and the things to be seized.
- [[Franks Challenges]] — the defendant's route to attack a warrant built on a knowing or reckless falsehood in the affidavit.
