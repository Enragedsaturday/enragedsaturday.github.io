# Standards of Proof

The shared vocabulary of certainty that every stop, arrest, and warrant is measured against: how much proof the law demands before an officer may act, and where each level sits on the ladder.

- [[The Proof Ladder]] — the full climb from a bare hunch through reasonable suspicion to probable cause, and where preponderance and proof beyond a reasonable doubt sit above them.
- [[Probable Cause and Reasonable Suspicion]] — the two working standards an officer applies in the field: what each requires, how they differ, and how a reviewing court checks them.
