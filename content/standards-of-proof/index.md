# Standards of Proof

The shared vocabulary of certainty that every stop, arrest, and warrant is measured against: how much proof the law demands before an officer may act, and where each level sits on the ladder.

- [[The Proof Ladder]] — the full climb from a bare hunch through reasonable suspicion to probable cause, and where preponderance and proof [[Common Legal Terms#beyond-a-reasonable-doubt|beyond a reasonable doubt]] sit above them.
- [[Reasonable Suspicion]] — the lower field standard: specific, articulable facts and rational inferences that authorize a brief investigative stop and a protective frisk.
- [[Probable Cause]] — the higher field standard: a fair probability, judged on the totality, that authorizes an arrest, a full search, or a warrant.
