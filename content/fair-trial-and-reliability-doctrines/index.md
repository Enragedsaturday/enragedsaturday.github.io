# Fair-Trial & Reliability Doctrines

Due-process doctrines that protect the reliability and fairness of the trial itself, grouped together because each guards the integrity of the evidence rather than the moment of search or seizure.

- [[Eyewitness Identification]] — the reliability limits on identification procedures, and the right to counsel at a post-charge lineup.
- [[Brady and Giglio]] — the prosecution's duty to disclose [[Brady and Giglio|exculpatory]] and impeachment evidence to the defense.
- [[Entrapment]] — the defense that arises when the government induces a person to commit a crime he was not predisposed to commit.
